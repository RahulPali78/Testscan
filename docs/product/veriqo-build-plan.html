01

The complete end-to-end product

All agreed application classes and cross-system workflows are committed implementation scope.

Product promise: customers connect supported applications and agents, define approved business expectations, execute complete cross-application workflows and receive independent outcome evidence and revision-bound release results.

Complete means the whole committed product

Web/API/agent execution is the foundation. Android/iOS, desktop, voice, private runners, self-service operation and production controls are required delivery work, not optional post-launch surfaces. Internal milestones are not general availability.

Representative end-to-end journeys

  • Mobile request → support agent → ticket API/state observer → customer web portal.
  • Desktop action → agent/tool workflow → backend record → another application confirmation.
  • Voice request → interruption/correction → agent handoff → authoritative action check and UI confirmation.

Support is defined by a published application/OS/browser/device/protocol matrix and approved adapters. All cells begin Planned. Accessible interfaces, valid app builds, authorized credentials, fixtures and observable outcomes are prerequisites; no claim of automatic compatibility with every system is implied.

Production scope includes authoring, datasets, scheduling, CLI/API, CI/CD, reporting, privacy, subscription/usage administration, support and offboarding. The implementation is complete only when all required classes and cross-application acceptance gates pass.

Deployable boundaries

Fargate isolates cloud tasks. Android/device and macOS/Windows/Linux interactive hosts are separate enrolled execution pools; they do not run inside Linux Fargate containers. Browser/device workers never receive the privileged state-reader credential. Customer-managed runners keep local credentials local and export only approved evidence.

Customer content stays out of Temporal payloads, search attributes and exceptions. Activities load scoped manifests and write artifacts to protected storage, returning opaque references. Pin runner/adapter versions, route compatible queued work and record additive schema migrations.

Adapter operationRequired meaning
capabilities()Declare supported platforms, actions, telemetry, observation, cancellation and safe retry guarantees.
prepare(context)Create/select namespaced fixture/session and return cleanup handle plus observed execution identity.
invoke(session, operation, input, limits)Execute approved bounded action with stable logical operation ID; emit validated evidence references.
observe(session, window)Use an authorized independent reader; include completeness, coverage and observation time/window.

Verdicts: a witnessed required failure yields FAIL; otherwise missing required evidence yields INCONCLUSIVE; PASS requires all mandatory checks on the attested candidate set. Explicit optional exclusions are NOT_TESTED and remain in coverage denominators. Unsupported mandatory capabilities reject publication or admission. Cleanup is a separate recorded dimension.

Retry and observation: orchestration deduplication does not guarantee one external business action. Reuse a target-enforced operation key or reconcile authoritative state; stop unsafe replay after an ambiguous response. Poll through the declared observation window rather than early-passing the first matching record. Record bounded evidence, not an unlimited exactly-once guarantee.

04

Nine milestones to the complete product

Evidence gates and dependency-based delivery replace the fixed six-sprint schedule.

Completion-based roadmap: nine engineering milestones deliver the complete committed product. Milestones are integration gates, not fixed two-week sprints. Web/agent execution is an early foundation; Android/iOS, desktop, voice, private runners and production operations remain required before general availability.

Capacity and forecast

Three experienced engineers plus a product/QA owner for 15–20 hours weekly. The 72 preliminary tasks total 348–581 engineering days. Reserve 30% of nominal capacity for integration, review and uncertainty. At 70% planned utilization, the optimistic aggregate capacity floor is 34 working weeks; unchanged role allocations create a 52-week lower bound. These are workload bounds, not delivery promises. Rebalance qualified engineers and re-estimate device/native/voice work before publishing calendar dates.

Milestone 1

Shared platform

Identity, versioned contracts, durable execution and the authenticated console.

Exit evidence: A scoped user runs a real synthetic action and independent checks distinguish correct, duplicate, missing and wrong-tenant outcomes.

Product / QA: Approve business expectations and matrix; maintain separate development and assessment fixtures.

Definition of ready

  • One accountable owner, explicit dependencies, a user-visible or operational deliverable and bounded required inputs.
  • Approved requirement and evidence source; no generated behavior automatically becomes the expected result.
  • Tenant, credential, network, device/OS permission and data-processing boundaries identified.

Definition of done

  • Reviewed implementation with passing relevant contract, outcome, negative, fault and compatibility checks.
  • Reproducible acceptance evidence, versioned manifests and updated operating/user documentation.
  • Observed usage and cleanup status; no placeholder telemetry, silent weakened assertions or unresolved critical findings.

Integrate small slices continuously; review shared contracts before dependent work. Keep one backlog across all milestones, record actual effort and reforecast when dependencies or skill assumptions change. AI coding tools can help with bounded implementation, but engineers and independent QA own access, migrations and business expectations.

Release authority

Product/QA and engineering owners approve a versioned evidence pack. Customer release policies start in shadow reporting and become blocking only under an authorized customer policy. Shadow mode is a release-integration safeguard, not a separate product tier or a substitute for complete-product readiness.

Reforecast triggers

  • Native automation, device provisioning or voice transport cannot meet the required contract without additional engineering.
  • Authoritative observation, cleanup or stable candidate identity is unavailable for a committed workflow.
  • Actual effort, role bottlenecks or critical findings consume planned reserve.
  • A new platform version, protocol or capability is proposed: add explicit tasks and acceptance evidence rather than hiding it in existing estimates.
